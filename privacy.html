Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 | Phantomix Ecosystem
Our Core Pledge: Phantomix is engineered with a local-first, zero-storage architecture. Your files, keys, and private data never hit our cloud servers.

1. Local-First Processing Architecture

Our web utilities—including Phantom-Shrink (Image Compressor), Phantom-Morph (Image Converter), Diamond-Vault (Password Generator), and Phantom-Wormhole (P2P File Transfer)—execute code directly inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript and WebRTC.

  • Your converted or compressed images are never uploaded to any central server.
  • P2P file transfers are direct device-to-device encrypted connections.
  • Password generation uses window.crypto API offline.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly minimize data collection:

  • Waitlist Email Addresses: If you voluntarily join our early access waitlist, your email is stored securely in our encrypted Supabase database solely for sending product launch updates.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: Non-identifiable diagnostic metrics (like server response ping times) may be evaluated locally to monitor system health.

3. Zero Data Selling

We do not, and will never, sell, rent, or trade user personal data to third-party ad networks or brokers. Phantomix is sustained through direct community support, micro-sponsorships, and future premium passes.

4. Data Security

All client-server communications (such as submitting feedback or waitlist requests) are transmitted over SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption. Data stored in our database uses AES-256 standards.

5. Your Rights & Support

You can request the deletion of your email from our waitlist database at any time by contacting our support team directly at phantomix.00@gmail.com.
